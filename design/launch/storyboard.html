The idea

We've all missed this Allow? before. The hook. Everyone who runs an AI coding agent knows it: you start it, go do something else, and come back 40 minutes later to find it stopped after 30 seconds to ask a question.

The reference works because it starts with something the viewer has done a hundred times ("we've all pressed this button"), shows it again and again until it hurts, then turns ("But… what if one button") and pays off. We keep that exact machine and feed it our truth:

0.0 – 3.3 sHook

"We've all missed this question before." A tiny Allow? on a Windows hill, then the punch zoom and the hand pressing it.

3.3 – 7.6 sBuild

19 fast cuts. The same question in 19 terminals, never moving on screen, while the small caption counts the wait: 5 seconds… 47 minutes.

7.6 – 12.5 sTurn

"But… what if one glance told you everything?" The island appears between the two famous hands. "Island."

12.5 – 22.9 sPayoff

No more endless alt-tabbing. No more forgotten agents. One click, and done. Agents. Answers. Tabs. Limits. Pure focus.

22.9 – 27.3 sName

The island's own thin line grows into the pill, Bloub opens his eyes. "All in one glance." Island. Free, for Windows.

What makes the reference feel expensive (and what we copy exactly)

  • The match cut. In the montage the button never moves or changes size. Only its skin and the world behind it change, five times a second. Ours: the question chip stays locked in the centre.
  • Small, then huge. It opens with tiny text on a calm landscape, then slams in 9× with motion blur. The contrast is the hook.
  • One word at a time. Words arrive with the voice, each with a short blur, the key word in colour and bold.
  • The hand. A cartoon cursor hand walks through almost every scene: it presses, underlines, pokes the letters. It is the viewer.
  • A new world for every line. Hills, cream paper, a painting, clean grey, black, grey again, a picture wall. No two lines share a look, so nothing feels like a slideshow.
  • Whip exits. Scenes leave with a blur or a one-frame colour flash, never a slow fade (only the two "breath" moments fade to white).
  • The logo is built, not shown. A line grows into the mark while the last line is spoken.

Creative touches that are only ours

  • The hills are Windows. The reference opens on green hills under a blue sky. For a Windows app that is the most famous desktop ever made, so our first frame already says "this is for your PC" without a word. (Painted by us, not the real wallpaper.)
  • The caption tells a second story. The reference has a tiny label under each button. Ours counts how long the agent has been waiting: [ asked 5 s ago ] … [ asked 47 min ago ]. People who pause the video get a joke.
  • The questions get desperate. Allow edit? → Run tests? → Push to main? → Still there? → Hello? → Anyone?
  • The reaction bubble is Bloub. Where the reference pops a heart, our mascot pops up and blinks.
  • The logo grows from the island's real folded state. The app rests as a thin white line at the top of the screen. The film's last move is that line opening into the pill.
  • The feature wall is the real app. The picture wall at "Pure focus" is made of the island's real screens, drawn by the app itself.
